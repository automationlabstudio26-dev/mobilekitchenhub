A 13,500 or 15,000 BTU rating describes cooling capacity, not electrical consumption. The exact running and starting demand comes from the selected unit’s documentation.

Cooling capacity ≠ watts

Two units with the same BTU rating can have different efficiency, voltage, compressor technology and fan demand. Compare rated current and locked-rotor or startup information.

Startup matters

Conventional compressors can require a brief starting current well above normal running current. Soft-start hardware may reduce peak demand, but compatibility and settings must be confirmed.

Real-world heat load

Cooking equipment, open service windows, insulation, climate and ventilation air can dominate the cooling requirement. Size the AC and electrical supply as one coordinated system.

The calculator’s 1,800 W default is a preliminary assumption, not a universal value for 13.5k or 15k BTU equipment.
