Menu-led checklist

Equipment categories to review

Cooking and hot holding

  • Fryers, griddles, ranges, ovens, grills or specialist appliances
  • Hot holding, warming and temperature monitoring
  • Heat-resistant clearances and secure mounting
  • Grease handling and safe shutdown access

Refrigeration and cold preparation

  • Reach-in, under-counter or preparation refrigeration
  • Freezer capacity where the menu requires it
  • Cold holding during travel, setup and peak service
  • Ventilation space and service access for condensers

Preparation, storage and service

  • Food-safe counters and preparation surfaces
  • Dry storage, shelving and secure travel restraints
  • Pass, service window, packaging and condiment stations
  • Point-of-sale, receipt, connectivity and charging needs

Handwashing and warewashing

  • Required handwash and utensil-washing sinks
  • Hot and cold water delivery
  • Potable-water and wastewater tank capacity
  • Soap, towels, drainage and clean-item storage

Ventilation and fire protection

  • Menu-led hood and exhaust assessment
  • Make-up air and heat management
  • Fire-suppression and extinguisher scope
  • Inspection, cleaning and service access

Power, gas and controls

  • Electrical panel, circuits, outlets and external connection
  • Generator or shore-power operating plan
  • LPG or gas storage, isolation and appliance supply
  • Lighting, pumps, fans, HVAC and monitoring loads
Equipment schedule

Record these details for every appliance

Identity and physical fit

Manufacturer, model, dimensions, weight, mounting, operating clearances, door swing and maintenance access.

Utility data

Voltage, phase, frequency, current or watts, manufacturer startup data, plug or connection, gas type, pressure, water and drainage.

Safety and approval data

Applicable product listings, installation instructions, ventilation classification, fire-protection scope and required certificates.

Layout checks

Make the equipment work as one system

  • Separate raw and ready-to-eat food workflows where required
  • Keep hot equipment away from refrigeration air intakes
  • Protect aisles, exits, controls and emergency shutoffs
  • Check axle loading, total payload and travel restraints
  • Allow safe working space for the expected staff count
  • Confirm cleaning access around and beneath equipment

Frequently asked questions

Food truck equipment questions

What equipment does a food truck need?

Equipment depends on the menu and service volume, but planning usually covers cooking, refrigeration, preparation, handwashing, warewashing, water, wastewater, storage, ventilation, fire protection, power or gas, lighting and point of sale.

How should I choose food truck appliances?

Choose model-specific commercial equipment using required output, dimensions, weight, voltage, phase, current or watts, startup data, gas type, ventilation needs, clearances, listings and service access.

Can I use domestic kitchen equipment in a food truck?

Do not assume domestic equipment will be accepted or suitable. Confirm applicable commercial-equipment, sanitation, safety and certification requirements with the responsible authorities and competent professionals before purchasing.

This checklist is general planning information. Equipment acceptance, installation and certification requirements vary by location and project. Confirm the final schedule with the relevant authorities, equipment manufacturers, fabricator and competent trades.
