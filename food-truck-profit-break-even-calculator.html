    Preliminary operating and break-even summary

      Open build-budget calculatorOpen startup guide

      Contribution comes first

      Every order must cover its ingredients, packaging and transaction fees before it contributes to labour, pitches, overhead and finance. A high sales figure can still produce weak cash.

      Test slow months

      Weather, seasonality, vehicle downtime and cancelled events can reduce trading days and orders. Keep a cash reserve and model conservative volume before accepting fixed commitments.

      Separate owner pay and tax

      Decide whether owner labour is included in daily wages. The result is before owner tax and may not equal accounting profit, distributable cash or lender-defined earnings.

      Validate before investing

      • Test average order value using an actual menu and realistic product mix.
      • Cost ingredients, packaging, waste and complimentary items recipe by recipe.
      • Include employer costs, setup, prep, cleaning and travel time in labour.
      • Model pitch commissions and event minimums separately where applicable.
      • Allow for repairs, maintenance, renewals and non-trading days.
      • Confirm taxes, depreciation, loan treatment and owner drawings with an accountant.
      • Compare actual weekly results with the model and update assumptions.
