Frequently asked questions

Perth food truck planning questions

What electrical supply is commonly used for a food truck in Perth?

The typical market supply is 230/400V, 50Hz, but the available service, phase, connector and protective devices must be confirmed for the vehicle and operating site.

Which approvals should I check before building in Perth?

Confirm food-business registration, street or event trading permission, fire review, gas requirements, vehicle rules and waste-water arrangements with the relevant authorities before committing to a build.

Can Mobile Kitchen Hub approve my final design?

No. The calculator and planning guide provide preliminary information only. Qualified trades, equipment manufacturers, the fabricator and local authorities must confirm the final design.

Western Australia approval pathway

Register the base, then notify each operating area

Register the mobile food business

WA Health says a mobile food van generally registers with the relevant local government where the vehicle is garaged overnight. The application may require design and fit-out information.

WA food-business registration guidance →

Notify other local governments

A mobile business operating across local-government boundaries may need to notify the other local governments where it intends to trade. Keep the registration certificate available and confirm location-specific conditions.

WA mobile food-business guidance →

Trading in the City of Perth

The City requires a Food Vendor Notification for food stalls or food trucks operating in the City, supported by food-business registration, insurance and a compliant site plan.

City of Perth food-vendor notification →

Preparing food at home

A home-based food business is not automatically covered by a food truck approval. WA Health lists food prepared in a residential home for sale as a food business requiring registration with the relevant local government. Confirm whether both the home premises and mobile unit form part of the approved operation.
