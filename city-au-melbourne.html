Choosing a mobile kitchen option in Melbourne

Custom food trailer

Suitable when you need a purpose-built layout, branded exterior and equipment package for ongoing operation. Compare payload, towing limits, service access and the complete compliance handover.

Food truck conversion

Combines the kitchen and vehicle in one unit. Confirm usable payload, ventilation routes, generator placement, maintenance access and how the finished vehicle will be inspected.

Mobile kitchen hire

Hiring can suit temporary events, renovations or demand testing. Confirm delivery, power, water, waste, cleaning, permits, insurance and the exact equipment supplied before booking.

Victoria approval pathway

Registration and trading checks before fabrication

Register or notify the food business

Victoria requires organisations selling food or drink to register or notify with a council. Your food activities and risk classification determine the applicable requirements.

Victoria Department of Health food-business guidance →

Record where and when you trade

Mobile and temporary food businesses use Victoria's FoodTrader service for registration or notification and statements of trade. Confirm the principal council and trading-location requirements before committing to a vehicle.

Open Victoria FoodTrader →

Coordinate gas and electrical work

Ask the fabricator for appliance data sheets, circuit schedules, gas installation records and testing documentation. Mobile catering vehicles require coordinated, compliant installations rather than separate equipment decisions.

Energy Safe Victoria mobile catering checklist →

Confirm the trading location

Food-business registration does not automatically grant permission to occupy every street, market or event site. Check landowner, event organiser and relevant council conditions for each intended location.

Frequently asked questions

Melbourne food truck planning questions

What electrical supply is commonly used for a food truck in Melbourne?

The typical market supply is 230/400V, 50Hz, but the available service, phase, connector and protective devices must be confirmed for the vehicle and operating site.

Which approvals should I check before building in Melbourne?

Confirm food-business registration, street or event trading permission, fire review, gas requirements, vehicle rules and waste-water arrangements with the relevant authorities before committing to a build.

Can Mobile Kitchen Hub approve my final design?

No. The calculator and planning guide provide preliminary information only. Qualified trades, equipment manufacturers, the fabricator and local authorities must confirm the final design.

Local requirements can change. This page, calculator and supplier links are planning resources—not regulatory approval, engineering design or a supplier guarantee.
