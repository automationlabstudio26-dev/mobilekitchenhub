    Preliminary equipment, payload and space brief

      Open layout plannerCheck axle distribution

      Payload is not gross vehicle rating

      Confirm what the chassis, body, trailer and fixed conversion already weigh. Available payload is the verified capacity left for the build elements and operating load you intend to add.

      Full tanks matter

      Water, wastewater, fuel, gas, batteries and stock can materially change loaded mass and axle reactions. Model the worst intended operating condition and do not count an allowance twice.

      Installed width is more than cabinet width

      Include required clearances, guards and the movement of doors or drawers when the occupied counter run is affected. Confirm maintenance access separately in the layout plan.

      Final verification

      Before fabrication or road use

      • Reconcile every schedule item against manufacturer documents and the builder quotation.
      • Confirm whether stands, accessories, liquids and mounting hardware are included in stated weights.
      • Check axle, tire, coupling and towing limits—not only total payload.
      • Coordinate equipment position with water tanks, batteries, generators, gas cylinders and stored product.
      • Verify counters, clearances, egress, ventilation, fire protection and utility access in the final drawings.
      • Obtain completed and loaded vehicle weights on appropriate scales.
