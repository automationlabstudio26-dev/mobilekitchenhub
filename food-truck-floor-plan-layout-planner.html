Builder-ready spatial brief

Preliminary layout summary

    Share project brief

    Use installed footprints

    Include side and rear clearances, guards, protruding handles, open-door or drawer movement and service access where relevant. Manufacturer cabinet dimensions alone may understate the space consumed.

    Coordinate the systems

    Equipment position affects ventilation, suppression nozzles, gas and electrical routing, water and drainage, axle loading, cleanability and staff movement. Freeze the appliance schedule before detailed fabrication.

    Check the loaded vehicle

    A floor plan does not prove payload or axle compliance. Add equipment, tanks, utilities, stored product and people to the separate weight-distribution calculation.

    Before fabrication

    Ask the builder to verify

    • Measured interior envelope, wall build-up and wheel-arch intrusions.
    • Required working, egress and equipment-service clearances.
    • Door, service-window and emergency-exit swing or access.
    • Ventilation capture, fire-suppression coverage and hot-equipment separation.
    • Electrical, gas, water, drainage and refrigeration routing.
    • Equipment restraint, structural reinforcement, loaded mass and axle reactions.
    • Food-safety, vehicle, fire, electrical and accessibility approvals in the operating jurisdiction.
