Quote preparation

What to send every food truck manufacturer

Commercial brief

  • Menu and expected service volume
  • Budget range and target date
  • Operating cities, events and climate
  • Truck, trailer or cart preference

Technical brief

  • Equipment schedule and manufacturer data
  • Electrical, gas, water and wastewater requirements
  • Ventilation and fire-protection assumptions
  • Payload, storage and staffing needs

Ask each builder to price the same written scope. Compare materials, named equipment, testing, approval support, delivery, warranty and exclusions—not the headline figure alone.
