Gas and LPG planning

Food truck gas installation and LPG safety planning

Coordinate propane, LPG or natural-gas appliances with the vehicle layout, ventilation, fire protection and local approval process before fabrication begins.

Open the planning checklistReview all equipment
Gas work is not a do-it-yourself task. Installation, alteration, testing and certification should be completed by a properly qualified or licensed professional where required. Requirements vary by jurisdiction.
Design inputs

Information the gas professional and fabricator need

Appliance schedule

  • Manufacturer, model and approved gas type
  • Input rating, pressure and connection information
  • Installation clearances and ventilation instructions
  • Flue, hood or exhaust requirements

Fuel supply

  • Expected simultaneous appliance demand
  • Cylinder or tank type, capacity and changeover plan
  • Regulator, pipework and isolation arrangement
  • Refilling, storage and transport procedure

Vehicle and enclosure

  • Proposed cylinder location and secure restraint
  • Ventilation openings and ignition-source separation
  • Protection from impact, heat and weather
  • Safe external access for isolation and servicing

Operating environment

  • Trading and travel modes
  • Doors, service openings and make-up air
  • Generator, batteries and electrical equipment nearby
  • Event, landowner and fire-authority conditions
System coordination

Gas, ventilation and fire protection must be designed together

Cooking equipment can affect heat load, grease-laden vapour, exhaust, make-up air, fire-suppression coverage and safe clearances. Changing an appliance after the hood or gas system is designed can invalidate assumptions across the whole installation.

Before handover

Inspection and documentation checklist

  • Confirm every appliance and component matches the approved design
  • Complete required pressure, tightness, operation and combustion-related checks
  • Verify cylinder restraints, ventilation and emergency isolation access
  • Confirm labels, warnings and operating instructions are installed
  • Collect certificates, test records, manuals and service intervals
  • Train operators in startup, shutdown, cylinder changes and emergency response
  • Record renewal or recurring inspection dates

Frequently asked questions

Mobile kitchen gas installation questions

Who should install gas equipment in a food truck?

Use a properly qualified or licensed gas professional as required in the operating jurisdiction. The installer should follow current local requirements and manufacturer instructions.

What records should I receive for a food truck gas installation?

Ask for the final appliance schedule, installation and test records, applicable certificates, operating instructions, emergency-isolation information and service requirements.

Can LPG cylinders be installed inside a food truck kitchen?

Cylinder location, enclosure, ventilation, restraints and separation requirements vary. Do not assume an interior location is permitted; have the design confirmed before fabrication.

This guide is not a gas-system design, installation instruction or certification. Verify current requirements with the relevant authorities, manufacturers, fabricator and appropriately qualified gas and fire-safety professionals.
