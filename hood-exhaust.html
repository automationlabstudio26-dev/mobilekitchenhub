Hood exhaust CFM and continuous electrical load

Airflow is selected for the hood, cooking duty and installation—not from electrical wattage alone. The fan motor then becomes a continuous load in the power plan.

Exhausting air creates replacement-air demand. Poor balance can reduce capture, pull conditioned air out of the vehicle and increase AC load.
