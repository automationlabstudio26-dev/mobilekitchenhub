      Compare like with like

      Ask each builder to separate base vehicle, fabrication, equipment, utility systems, approvals, delivery and taxes. Record exclusions and owner-supplied items beside every quote.

      Protect the contingency

      Contingency is for unresolved scope and genuine surprises. It should not hide missing known items, underpriced equipment or expected design changes.

      Plan cash timing

      A fully funded total can still create a cash-flow problem. Confirm deposits, progress payments, equipment orders, retention, commissioning and final handover milestones.

      Before accepting a quotation

      • Confirm the exact chassis, trailer or vehicle specification and ownership status.
      • Reconcile every appliance and utility item against drawings and schedules.
      • Identify permits, professional fees, inspections and authority charges.
      • Clarify delivery, registration, insurance, warranties and after-sales support.
      • Document payment milestones, change-order rules and cancellation terms.
      • Check which taxes are included and whether any are recoverable.
      • Keep working capital and launch costs separate from the fabrication budget.
