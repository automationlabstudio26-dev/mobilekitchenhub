How we use your information

This page explains the information processed when you request builder quotes through Mobile Kitchen Hub.

Information collected

Name, email address, location, country/market, equipment selections, custom notes, calculator results, consent and submission time.

Purpose

We use this information to respond, manage the enquiry and share it with relevant commercial mobile-kitchen builders so they can contact you about your project.

Service providers

Netlify may host the site; Make may route submissions; Airtable may store lead records; and Google services may deliver notifications. These providers process information for the stated workflow.

Analytics and measurement

We may record privacy-safe events such as calculator starts, CTA clicks, validation errors and completed submissions to understand site performance. These analytics events must not contain names, email addresses, location text, equipment notes or other form details.

Your choices

Do not submit the form if you do not agree to this use. To request access, correction or deletion, email automationlabstudio26@gmail.com.

Mobile Kitchen Hub does not sell submitted information for unrelated marketing. This notice should be reviewed whenever the lead-routing workflow or providers change.
