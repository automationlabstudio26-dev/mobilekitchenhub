Food truck build and inspection checklist

A printable starting point for organizing your concept, equipment, utilities, approvals and handover documents.

Concept and vehicle

Define menu and expected service volume
Confirm truck or trailer dimensions and payload
Measure doors, aisles, counters and equipment clearances
Plan staff workflow and customer service openings

Equipment and utilities

Collect model-specific cut sheets
Record voltage, phase, running watts and startup data
Confirm gas type and appliance listings
Size fresh-water and waste-water systems

Safety and approvals

Confirm food-business and trading requirements
Review hood and fire-suppression scope
Confirm electrical and gas inspections
Record required vehicle and weight checks

Builder handover

Receive final equipment and panel schedules
Collect warranties and operating manuals
Document testing and inspection results
Photograph concealed utilities before closure
This checklist is general planning information. Local requirements and project specifications must be confirmed with qualified professionals and the relevant authorities.
