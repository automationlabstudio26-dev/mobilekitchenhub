Builder-ready scope

Six systems that must work together

Espresso production

State group count, steam demand, peak drinks per hour, grinder count, batch-brew or hot-water needs and the exact proposed models.

Electrical supply

Record voltage, phase, maximum wattage and current for every appliance. Include simultaneous loads, motor startup and planned shore, generator or battery sources.

Water treatment

Plan potable storage, filtration or treatment, pump pressure, drainage, wastewater capacity, cleaning and winterization where relevant.

Cold chain

Allow for milk refrigeration, display storage, ice, ambient conditions, door openings and safe temperature monitoring throughout service.

Barista workflow

Map order taking, grinding, extraction, steaming, handwashing, rinsing, pickup and restocking so people and hot liquids do not conflict.

Vehicle and service

Check payload, axle distribution, ventilation, equipment restraints, access panels, maintenance clearances and local vehicle requirements.

Power reality

The espresso machine may be the largest continuous load

Commercial machine ratings vary significantly by model and configuration. For example, La Marzocco publishes 220–240 V ratings from 2.5 kW for a one-group Linea Classic S, while larger configurations can exceed 5 kW. Use the selected model’s nameplate and installation documents—never the example alone.

Add every service load

  • Espresso machine and grinders
  • Water heater, pump and treatment controls
  • Milk refrigerator, display fridge and ice maker
  • Blender, batch brewer or hot-water equipment
  • Lighting, POS, chargers and ventilation

Design for the operating pattern

Boiler recovery, steaming, refrigeration cycling and other appliances can overlap. A builder or electrician needs both the connected load and the realistic simultaneous-demand plan.

Prepare your enquiry

Send a brief a regional fabricator can price

  1. Operating city or region and intended trading locations.
  2. Vehicle, trailer or kiosk preference and target dimensions.
  3. Menu, expected peak drinks per hour and staffing.
  4. Exact appliance schedule with manufacturer specification sheets.
  5. Preferred shore, generator, battery or hybrid power approach.
  6. Potable water, wastewater and cleaning expectations.
  7. Budget range, target launch date and required approvals.
