Define the same scope for every fabricator

Menu and service model

  • Menu, batch sizes and meals per service
  • Prep, cooking, hot-holding and refrigeration workflow
  • Staff numbers and customer service arrangement
  • Intended markets, events and trading locations

Trailer and payload

  • Target body length, width and internal headroom
  • Tow vehicle capacity and intended aggregate mass
  • Equipment, water, LPG, stock and staff weight allowances
  • Axle, drawbar, braking and stability assumptions

Utilities and safety systems

  • 230/400V, 50Hz appliance schedule and simultaneous load
  • Shore power, generator and changeover requirements
  • Gas appliances, cylinder storage and installation records
  • Hood, exhaust, make-up air and fire protection

Handover package

  • Equipment manuals, serial numbers and warranties
  • Electrical and gas test documentation
  • Materials, finishes and cleaning specifications
  • As-built drawings and inspection support

Questions to ask a custom food trailer builder

Relevant experience

Ask for recent builds with a comparable menu, equipment package and operating model. Contact references and inspect a completed unit where practical.

What is included?

Confirm the chassis, body, finishes, equipment, installation, graphics, registration support, delivery, testing and documentation included in the written price.

Change control

Record how design changes, substitutions, delays and additional work are approved and priced. Tie payments to defined milestones and evidence of progress.

Service and warranty

Clarify defects, component warranties, response times, travel charges and who coordinates appliance, electrical, gas or fabrication warranty claims.

Frequently asked questions

Melbourne food trailer builder questions

What should I send a Melbourne food trailer builder for an accurate quotation?

Provide the menu, service volume, trailer size, equipment schedule, appliance data sheets, preferred fuel, electrical load, water and wastewater needs, ventilation requirements, budget, timeline and intended trading locations.

How should I compare custom food trailer quotations?

Compare the same written scope, inclusions, exclusions, materials, equipment models, payload assumptions, testing, compliance documents, warranty, payment stages and delivery terms rather than headline price alone.

Does Mobile Kitchen Hub certify the listed fabricators?

No. Listings are regional research options. Verify licences, insurance, references, past builds, warranties and approval support directly before paying a deposit.
