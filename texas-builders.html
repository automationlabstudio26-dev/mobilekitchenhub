Prepare the electrical brief

  • List every cooking, refrigeration, HVAC, pump and lighting load.
  • Record voltage, phase, running watts and manufacturer startup data.
  • Separate generator loads from shore-power-only equipment.
  • Ask the fabricator how the generator compartment is ventilated and isolated.

Texas licensing checkpoint

Texas DSHS introduced statewide mobile food vendor licensing changes beginning July 1, 2026. Licensing category and inspection needs depend on food-preparation activities; local fire, zoning and operational rules can still apply.

Review Texas DSHS mobile food vendor guidance.

Questions for builders

  • Can you provide a panel schedule and load calculation?
  • What shore connector and generator output are proposed?
  • How are HVAC, hood, refrigeration and water heating sequenced?
  • Which inspection documents are included at handover?
