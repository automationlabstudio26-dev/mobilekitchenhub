Nameplate BTU/h

Use the manufacturer rating plate or manual—not a generic appliance estimate. Add every appliance that can operate simultaneously.

Duty cycle

Appliances normally cycle rather than fire at full input for an entire shift. The duty-cycle field estimates fuel consumption; connected load still matters for supply design.

Cylinders and vaporization

Fuel volume alone does not prove that a cylinder can vaporize enough propane in cold weather. Have the complete supply checked professionally.

Calculation and official reference

The shift estimate is connected BTU/h × hours × duty cycle × reserve ÷ 91,452 BTU per gallon. The conversion is an approximate planning factor published by the U.S. Energy Information Administration.
