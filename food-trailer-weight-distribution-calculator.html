Loading principles vary by vehicle and jurisdiction

United Kingdom

The Highway Code says not to overload the vehicle or trailer, to distribute weight with heavy items over the axle(s), and not to exceed the manufacturer’s tow-ball recommendation.

Australia

Transport WA defines ATM, GTM, payload and tow-ball mass, and stresses manufacturer towing, combination and axle limits.

Frequently asked questions

Food trailer loading questions

How does equipment position affect tongue weight?

Weight ahead of the axle group generally increases downward tongue load; weight behind it reduces tongue load. Both the weight and distance from the axle matter.

Can the total trailer be within its limit while an axle is overloaded?

Yes. Overall mass, axle-group load, coupling load, tow-vehicle ratings and combination limits are separate constraints and must each be verified.

Can this calculator approve a trailer for road use?

No. It is a simplified static estimate. Qualified specialists must confirm structure, ratings, stability, braking, tyres and legal compliance.

Safety limitation: This simplified two-support static model does not predict dynamic stability, side-to-side balance, individual-axle sharing, suspension behaviour, tyre loads, braking or structural strength. Do not alter or operate a trailer from this estimate alone. Verify the loaded unit on suitable scales and obtain manufacturer or engineering approval.
