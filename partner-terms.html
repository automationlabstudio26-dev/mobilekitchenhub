Partner subscription terms

The practical terms for receiving structured buyer enquiries through Mobile Kitchen Hub.

Effective 21 October 2026

1. Business eligibility

Partner subscriptions are intended for food-truck builders, fabricators, conversion specialists and related commercial service providers. We may ask for business, service-area, insurance, licence or reference information before routing enquiries.

2. What counts as a lead

A lead is a buyer enquiry containing contact information and project details that broadly match the service area and criteria agreed with the partner. A lead is an opportunity to contact a prospective customer—not a guaranteed sale, signed project or confirmed budget.

3. Package delivery

Package quantities are monthly targets based on suitable enquiry availability. Before paid routing begins, we will confirm your regions and criteria in writing. If suitable volume is lower than the package target, we will agree in writing to carry the shortfall forward, extend service or apply another fair remedy.

4. Replacement requests

Request review within seven days of delivery if a lead has invalid contact details, is a duplicate previously supplied by us, or clearly falls outside the agreed region or criteria. Non-response, a changed mind, an unaccepted quote or a lead that does not become a sale does not by itself qualify for replacement.

5. Billing and renewal

Paid packages are billed monthly in advance through Stripe and renew automatically until cancelled. Prices and taxes shown at checkout apply. A subscription does not create exclusivity unless we confirm exclusive routing separately in writing.

6. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel before the next billing date to stop future renewal. Fees already paid are normally non-refundable once the subscription period has begun or leads have been delivered, except where required by law or where we agree another remedy under section 3.

7. Partner responsibilities

You are responsible for promptly and lawfully contacting leads, providing accurate quotations, safeguarding personal data, and maintaining all licences, insurance and standards required for your work. You must not make misleading claims or use a lead's details for unrelated marketing.

8. Platform role

Mobile Kitchen Hub introduces buyers and regional fabricators using information supplied by each party. We do not certify every listed business, perform construction work, guarantee project outcomes or act as a party to contracts between buyers and partners.

9. Privacy

Lead information must be handled only for the requested project and in accordance with applicable privacy and marketing laws. Our Privacy Policy explains how Mobile Kitchen Hub handles submitted information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms prospectively and will provide reasonable notice of material subscription changes. Questions, cancellation requests and lead reviews can be sent to automationlabstudio26@gmail.com.

Questions before subscribing?

Ask us to confirm your target region, lead criteria and expected routing before you start.
