Plan review and licensing

Florida DBPR provides a Mobile Food Dispensing Vehicle guide and plan-review pathway. A licensed unit needs suitable food protection, refrigeration, power, plumbing and washing facilities.

Cooling changes the load profile

Rooftop air conditioning, refrigeration and ice production can overlap for long periods. Ask for manufacturer startup data and confirm whether the proposed generator can handle simultaneous motor starts.

Fabricator questions

  • How is heat rejected around the condenser and generator?
  • Is the panel schedule based on measured or nameplate loads?
  • What shore power is supported?
  • Which drawings and test records are supplied?
