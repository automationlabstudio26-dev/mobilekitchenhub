Commissary and servicing

The Dallas guidance describes a permitted commissary as the operating base for cleaning, servicing, potable water, wastewater, supplies and overnight storage. Confirm the arrangement before fabrication.

Plan review

A new, converted, remodelled or ownership-changed unit may require plan review. Prepare a menu, scaled layout, equipment data, finishes and utility details for authority and builder discussions.

Beyond Dallas

Fort Worth and surrounding cities may use different health, fire, zoning and vending processes. Check the exact operating address rather than treating the Metroplex as one permit area.

Frequently asked questions

DFW mobile kitchen questions

What electrical supply is commonly used for a food truck in Dallas–Fort Worth?

Commercial mobile kitchens commonly use 120/240V, 60Hz equipment, but confirm the final voltage, phase, connectors, service capacity and protective devices for the unit and every operating site.

Does one permit cover every Dallas–Fort Worth jurisdiction?

Do not assume so. Health, fire, zoning, vending and event requirements can differ between Dallas, Fort Worth and surrounding municipalities.

What should be confirmed before ordering a truck or trailer?

Confirm the menu, servicing base, locations, plan-review pathway, equipment schedule, payload, ventilation, fire protection, gas, electrical and water systems.

Requirements change and vary by jurisdiction. This guide and calculator provide preliminary planning information—not regulatory approval or engineering design. Confirm the final pathway with the relevant authorities and qualified trades.
