Common questions

Project-brief and matching FAQ

Is this free for operators?

Yes. The calculator and project-brief submission do not require an operator subscription or oblige you to buy.

Will I definitely receive a quote?

No. We may match a suitable, consented brief with relevant regional fabricators, but availability, responses and quotations are not guaranteed.

What information may be shared?

With your consent, your contact details, location, menu concept, equipment selections, preliminary power estimate, project stage, budget band, vehicle size and target date may be shared with relevant fabricators.

Is the calculator result a final electrical design?

No. It is a preliminary planning estimate. Confirm ratings, surge, voltage, phase, fuel, local codes and final design with qualified local professionals.
