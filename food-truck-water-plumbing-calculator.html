      How the estimate works

      Transparent sizing assumptions

      OutputPreliminary methodFinal check
      Estimated useService volume × water per service, plus handwash, cleaning and other process allowances.Observe a comparable operation or use fixture flow data.
      Fresh-water capacityEstimated use plus the entered reserve percentage.Local minimum capacity, refill access and usable tank volume.
      Wastewater capacityFresh-water capacity × entered wastewater factor.Authority-required ratio and permitted disposal method.
      Heater recoveryHot-water share divided across service hours.Fixture peak, incoming temperature, delivery temperature and heater rating.
      Water payloadApproximately 1 kg/L or 8.34 lb/US gal.Full-tank axle loads and the completed vehicle's measured weight.

      Fresh-water tank

      Specify food-safe materials, secure mounts, screened venting, accessible filling, drain-down and cleaning access. Confirm the pump's flow and pressure against all fixtures.

      Wastewater tank

      Plan for overflow prevention, approved disposal, service access and tank venting. Wastewater location changes axle loading as the fresh tank empties.

      Hot-water system

      Coordinate the heater's recovery rate, storage, fuel or electrical load, ventilation and safe discharge. A nominal tank volume alone does not prove peak performance.

      Weight scenario warning: the “full-system planning mass” conservatively adds full fresh and full wastewater volumes plus the entered dry-system allowance. Actual operating states differ, but the builder should test credible worst-case tank combinations at each axle.

      Information to confirm before fabrication

      • Local minimum fresh-water and wastewater capacities and required ratio.
      • Required sinks, basin dimensions, drainboards, taps, backflow protection and air gaps.
      • Hot-water delivery temperature, recovery rate and heater installation rules.
      • Food-safe tank, hose, pump and pipe specifications.
      • Tank mounting, vents, fill points, level indication, drains and winterisation.
      • Full and partially full tank effects on payload, axles and stability.
      • Approved filling and wastewater disposal locations.
