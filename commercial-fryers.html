Equipment power guide

Commercial fryer wattage and generator planning

Electric fryers are often among the largest steady loads in a mobile kitchen. Use the nameplate—not a generic web figure—for final planning.

Add a fryer to my estimate

Running load

Heating elements are primarily resistive loads, but controls, filtration pumps and supporting equipment can add demand. A double fryer may require multiple circuits or a higher-voltage supply.

Surge terminology

Unlike compressors and motors, heating elements generally do not create the same starting surge. The design challenge is sustained current, simultaneous cooking demand and any auxiliary motors.

Specification checklist

  • Rated voltage, phase and frequency
  • Rated input in watts or amps
  • Plug/receptacle or hard-wire requirement
  • Duty cycle and simultaneous-load assumptions
  • Hood and suppression classification
