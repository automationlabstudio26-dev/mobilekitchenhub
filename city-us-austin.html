Frequently asked questions

Austin food trailer questions

Who issues Austin mobile food vendor permits in 2026?

Austin Public Health states that Texas DSHS administers the state Mobile Food Vending Permit beginning July 1, 2026. Use the current city and state guidance because transition details may evolve.

When does an Austin food trailer need a fire inspection?

Austin Fire states that its process applies to units with propane or appliances, including electric appliances, that produce grease-laden vapors. Confirm the current requirement for the proposed equipment and location.

Does a mobile food permit grant permission to use any Austin site?

No. Health, fire, zoning, private-property, event and public-right-of-way permissions are separate.

Permit administration changed in 2026 and may continue to evolve. Verify current requirements directly with Texas DSHS, Austin Fire, the property or event authority and qualified trades before committing to a build.
