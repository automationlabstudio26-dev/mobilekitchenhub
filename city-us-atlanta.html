Frequently asked questions

Atlanta mobile kitchen questions

What approvals should an Atlanta food truck operator investigate?

Investigate county health permits and base-of-operation requirements, authorization for other counties, City of Atlanta business and vending requirements, property or event permission, and applicable fire review.

Does a Georgia mobile food permit allow vending anywhere in Atlanta?

No. Health approval and permission to occupy or vend from a location are separate. Public right-of-way, private property and events can require different permissions.

What electrical standard is common for Atlanta mobile kitchens?

Equipment commonly uses 120/240V, 60Hz supply, but confirm voltage, phase, current, connectors, generator rating and site power for the final package.
