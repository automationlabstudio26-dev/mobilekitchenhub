Interpret the result

Energy capacity and inverter power are different constraints

Battery kWh

Energy capacity determines how long the load can run. Refrigeration cycling, preparation periods and changing menu demand should be reflected in the average-load assumption.

Inverter kW

The inverter must support simultaneous continuous demand and short peaks. Motor starts, induction equipment and resistive cooking loads can dominate the design.

Charging power

Solar roof area may be limited. Shore charging, alternator charging or a hybrid generator can be needed to restore the battery reliably between services.

Frequently asked questions

Mobile-kitchen battery questions

How many kWh of battery storage does a food truck need?

It depends on average simultaneous load, operating hours, losses, reserve and usable depth of discharge. Use equipment data and observed duty cycles rather than vehicle size alone.

Can a food truck run entirely from batteries?

Some lower-load operations can, but electric cooking can make the battery, inverter, charging and thermal-management system very large. Hybrid operation may be more practical for some menus.

Does this calculator design a compliant lithium system?

No. It is a preliminary energy estimate. Qualified specialists must confirm the modules, protection, isolation, wiring, ventilation, fire safety, charging and applicable requirements.

Safety limitation: High-energy lithium systems can create electric-shock, arc-flash, fire, thermal-runaway and vehicle-safety hazards. Do not purchase or assemble a system from this result alone. Use qualified battery, electrical and vehicle specialists and follow applicable manufacturer and authority requirements.
