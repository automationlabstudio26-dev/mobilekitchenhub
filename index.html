Privacy

How we use your information

When you share a project brief, Mobile Kitchen Hub collects the name, email address, location, equipment selections, and calculator estimate you submit. We use this information to respond to your request, manage it in our lead database, and share it with relevant regional fabricators or participating builders so they can contact you about your project. Our service providers—including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Make, Airtable, and Google—may process this information to host the site, route submissions, store requests, and deliver notifications. We do not sell your information for unrelated marketing. To request access, correction, or deletion of your submission, email automationlabstudio26@gmail.com.
